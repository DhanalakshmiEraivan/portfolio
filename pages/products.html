02 · How we build

Small tools, made to be kept.

These are the product principles we argue about when a feature is tempting but noisy.

Built in-house

Ideas, interface and engineering stay inside the studio, so decisions do not get lost between teams.

Quiet by default

No upsell pop-ups, no dark patterns. The product does the job and then gets out of the way.

Clear language

Labels and messages say what happened and what to do next, in words a first-time user can follow.

Maintained

Products are treated as long-lived work — updates, fixes and small refinements after launch.
