06 · Questions

Answers before you commit.

The six questions we are asked most often. If yours is missing, ask it directly.

How long does website development take?
It depends on the scope. A focused marketing site usually takes several weeks from kickoff to launch; custom web applications take longer because the flows, data and edge cases need more design and testing. We agree a realistic sequence before the build starts.
Will my website work on mobile devices?
Yes. Every page is designed and tested across small phones, tablets and desktop widths. Mobile is treated as a primary layout, not a shrunken afterthought.
Can you redesign my existing website?
Yes. We start by auditing what already works — content, structure, search visibility — so a redesign keeps the value you have built and fixes the parts that are holding it back.
Can you integrate APIs and databases?
Yes. We connect forms, payments, CRM tools, analytics and custom APIs, and we design the data model so the site keeps behaving as content and traffic grow.
Will my website be SEO-friendly?
We build clean semantic structure, sensible page titles and metadata, fast-loading pages and crawlable content. Content strategy and ongoing publishing still matter, and we will be honest about what sits outside the build.
Can I request future updates and maintenance?
Yes. Many clients continue with a light maintenance rhythm after launch: updates, fixes, new pages and small improvements. There is no obligation to do that with us, and handover documentation makes it easy either way.
