03 · Process

Small steps, honest limits.

We do not promise magic. We design automations that fail visibly and can be switched off.

STAGE 01

Understand

Which task is slow, repetitive or error-prone — and what it costs today.

STAGE 02

Analyze

Inputs, decisions and exceptions mapped before any model is chosen.

STAGE 03

Design

The workflow, the review point and the fallback when confidence is low.

STAGE 04

Implement

Built into the tools your team already uses, with permissions respected.

STAGE 05

Monitor

Accuracy, time saved and failures watched — with a switch to pause it all.
