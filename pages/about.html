01 The studio

Built around a shipping habit

QuadraFroyn Solutions started with a simple frustration: too much software is planned beautifully and finished badly. So we built our own products to keep ourselves honest about the whole lifecycle — discovery, design, engineering, launch and the quiet maintenance that follows.

Our own tools, Quadraconverter and Quadra AI Resume Builder, are where we test ideas about clarity, speed and respect for the people using them. Client work gets the same standard, with none of the theatre.

  • One team from first sketch to production support
  • Product decisions explained in plain language
  • Documentation and handover treated as part of the build
  • No invented case studies, logos or testimonials

02 How we think

Three values we can defend

They are not wall posters. They are the arguments we keep having when a project gets difficult.

Say the simple thing first

Every proposal starts with a one-page problem statement: who is stuck, what changes if we fix it, and what we are deliberately not doing. If that page is weak, no amount of design polish will save the project.

What you get

  • One-page problem statement
  • Explicit out-of-scope list
  • Decision log you can read
  • Plain-language milestones

Details are the product

Spacing, error states, empty states, loading behaviour, keyboard focus. The small decisions are what people actually feel. We review them together instead of treating polish as a final coat of paint.

What you get

  • Design and code reviews
  • Accessibility checks
  • Performance budgets
  • QA before every release

Bad news travels fast

If a timeline is slipping or an idea is weaker than it looked, you hear it early with options. We would rather have an uncomfortable Tuesday than a surprise launch week.

What you get

  • Weekly written updates
  • Risks raised with options
  • No inflated promises
  • Direct access to the makers

03 Disciplines

Six crafts, one table

Strategy, design and engineering sit together. Fewer handoffs, fewer telephone games, faster decisions.

Product strategy

Framing the problem, choosing the smallest useful version, and deciding what success should look like before a line of code.

Interface design

Flows, wireframes, visual systems and prototypes that engineers can actually build without guesswork.

Front-end engineering

Accessible, responsive interfaces with careful motion, sensible components and performance budgets.

Back end & platform

APIs, data models, authentication and infrastructure designed to survive the second year, not just the demo.

Mobile engineering

iOS, Android and cross-platform apps with offline thinking, store readiness and a plan for updates.

AI & automation

Practical assistants and workflows with human review, clear limits and measurable usefulness.

04 Process

A calm path from idea to live

Four visible phases. Each one ends with something you can read, click or test — never a status meeting in search of a deliverable.

PHASE 01

Discover

Interviews, constraints and a one-page brief. We leave with a shared problem statement and a rough shape for the solution.

PHASE 02

Design

Flows, prototypes and a visual direction. You react to something real, not a document describing something real.

PHASE 03

Build

Short cycles, working software and weekly notes. Risks surface while there is still room to change course.

PHASE 04

Care

Launch checks, monitoring, documentation and a support rhythm. The product keeps working after the applause.

05 Questions

The honest answers

Is QuadraFroyn a design agency or an engineering company?
Both, deliberately. The same team designs and builds, so decisions do not get lost in a handoff. We also run our own products, which keeps the engineering honest.
Do you work with early-stage founders?
Yes, when the problem is clear enough to scope. We are happy to start with a short discovery engagement before committing to a full build.
Who owns the code and design files?
You do, on final payment. We hand over repositories, design sources, documentation and environment notes as part of the engagement.
Are the products and clients on this site real?
Quadraconverter and Quadra AI Resume Builder are real QuadraFroyn products. Other imagery, quotes and role profiles on this concept portfolio are illustrative and labelled as such.
